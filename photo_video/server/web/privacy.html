Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026

Reel (“the app”) scores photos you provide and assembles the best of them into a short slideshow video. This policy explains what the app does with the images you upload. It is written to reflect how the app actually works.

Operator: this template describes Reel's real data flow. Before publishing, replace the [bracketed] placeholders with your details. Google Play requires a working contact and a reachable privacy-policy URL.

What we collect

  • Photos you upload — the images you choose to score or turn into a slideshow.
  • Render settings you set (e.g. resolution, number of photos, scoring weights). These are plain preferences, not personal data.

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no advertising. It does not ask for your name, email, location, or contacts.

How your photos are used

  • Uploaded images are sent to the server only to fulfil your request — to compute quality scores, detect subjects, and render a slideshow.
  • Processing is automated. Your photos are not used to train any model, are not sold, and are not shared with third parties.

Retention

  • Uploaded images are held only in memory for the duration of the request and are not written to long-term storage.
  • Rendered slideshow videos are stored temporarily so you can download them, in ephemeral server storage that is cleared automatically when the server instance recycles. They are meant to be downloaded right away and are not retained long-term.

On your device

The app is an installable web app. A service worker caches the interface files (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, icons) on your device so it loads quickly and works offline. This cache stays on your device, contains no personal data, and can be cleared from your browser or by uninstalling. The app sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

Third parties & hosting

The service is hosted on cloud infrastructure ([your hosting provider, e.g. Google Cloud Run]), which processes requests on the operator's behalf. No analytics or advertising SDKs are included.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You control what you upload — don't upload a photo you don't want processed. Because nothing is stored under an account, there is no profile to delete; clearing the app's cache removes the on-device copy of the interface. For any question about this policy, contact [operator contact email].
